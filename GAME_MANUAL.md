# Dungeon Brawl: The Ultimate Game Manual

Welcome to **Dungeon Brawl**, a strategic turn-based game that combines the trivia challenge of Jeopardy with the chaos of a card battler and RPG progression systems. This manual covers everything you need to know to play, survive, and win.

---

## 1. Game Overview

**Objective:** The player with the highest score when the last question is answered wins the game.

**Core Gameplay:**
*   Players take turns selecting questions from a board (Categories & Point Values).
*   Correct answers award points. Wrong answers deduct points.
*   Players use **Cards** and **Actions** to manipulate scores, steal from opponents, gain advantages, and cause chaos.
*   **Quests** provide side objectives for massive rewards.

---

## 2. Game Setup: The Role of the Game Master

**Dungeon Brawl** is designed to be played as a social "party game" experience, best enjoyed on a large shared screen (TV or Projector).

*   **The Game Master (GM):** One person acts as the host and controls the computer running the game. Their job is to:
    *   Navigate the UI (clicking tiles, activating cards) based on the players' verbal instructions.
    *   Read the questions aloud to the room.
    *   Act as the final judge on whether a spoken answer is "Correct" or "Wrong".
    *   Keep the pace of the game moving and narrate the chaos!

*   **The Teams (Players):** The other participants form teams (or play individually).
    *   Teams sit together facing the screen.
    *   When it is a team's turn, they deliberate and tell the GM what to do (e.g., *"We'll take 'History' for 400"*, *"Activate the Loot Goblin against Team Red!"*).
    *   The GM executes the command, and the game proceeds.

This setup allows for a lively, game-show atmosphere where negotiation, bluffing, and table-talk are just as important as the mechanics on the screen.

## 3. Turn Structure

A player's turn consists of the following phases:

1.  **Turn Start:**
    *   Passive card effects trigger (e.g., *Niffler* gives points, *Tick* drains points).
    *   "Black Market" draw: You may be offered a choice of random cards to add to your inventory.
2.  **Action Phase (Pre-Question):**
    *   You can use **Cards** from your inventory.
    *   You can perform **Dungeon Actions** (buttons on the side) like buying cards or sacrificing health.
    *   You can **Feed the Spider** to upgrade your Spider Sense.
3.  **Selection Phase:**
    *   Select an available tile (Question) from the board.
    *   *Note: If you are "Puppet Locked", you may be forced to choose a specific category.*
4.  **Question Phase:**
    *   The question is revealed.
    *   **Answer**: Reveal the answer and mark yourself as "Correct" or "Wrong".
    *   **Pass**: You can choose to pass on a question to avoid the penalty of a wrong answer, but you gain no points.
5.  **Turn End:**
    *   Scores are updated based on the question result.
    *   Play passes to the next player.

## 4. Dungeon Actions

Located on the side of the board, these actions are always available (unless frozen by an enemy). Most have an "Upgraded" version unlockable via Quests.

| Action | Cost / Trigger | Effect | Upgraded Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Card Jester** | Pay 200 Points | Buy a random card. | Buy a card for **100 Points** (50% discount). |
| **Mad Seer** | Pay 100 Points | Reveal a random hidden tile's question/answer without selecting it. | Reveal a tile for **Free**. |
| **Blood Sacrifice** | Lose 100 Points | Gain a random card. | Lose **50 Points** to gain a card. |
| **Frog of Fate** | Free (1/turn) | 50% chance to gain 100 pts. 50% chance to lose 100 pts. | Gain **200 pts** / Lose **50 pts**. |
| **Golden Idol** | Free (Global) | A global bonus pot grows every turn. Click to claim it, but it resets to 0 for everyone. | You get **Double** the current pot value. |
| **The Spider** | Feed Items | Drag specific items (*Isopod*, *Sheep*) to the spider to level up **Spider Sense**. | Higher Spider Sense = More bonus points per question & more Rerolls. |

This section lists every known card in the dungeon and its specific effects.

*These cards automatically trigger effects at the start or end of your turn just by being in your inventory.*

| Card Name | Effect Description |
| :--- | :--- |

| **Cursed Coin** | A coin imbued with dark magic. You receive **500 points** when acquired, but lose **50 points** each turn for 10 turns. |
| **Infinite Money Glitch** | You've discovered an exploit in reality itself. Passively gain **1% of your current points** every turn. |
| **Niffler** | This little critter has a nose for gold. Passively gain **25 points** every turn. |
| **Tick** | A parasitic creature that latches onto your soul. Each turn it drains **1% of your current points**. (Try to get rid of this!) |

### Active Cards (Offensive & Utility)
*These cards must be manually activated from your inventory during your Action Phase. Most are consumed (discarded) upon use.*

| Card Name | Effect Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Coalition** | Forge a temporary **Alliance** with another player. While allied, you cannot target each other with harmful effects. Lasts for several turns. |
| **Glacial Elemental** | Command the power of ice. Activate to **Freeze** a tile (making it unselectable) or a dungeon Action (disabling it) until your next turn. |
| **Loot Goblin** | A greedy goblin does your dirty work. Activate to **steal 200 points** from a player of your choice. |
| **Martin** | Martin the quest-giver bestows upon you a sacred **Quest**. Complete the objective for great rewards including cards, gold, and action upgrades. |
| **Puppet Master** | Dark strings bind your enemy's will. Activate to force an opponent to only answer questions from a **Category** of your choice on their next turn. |
| **Roulette** | Stake up to **500 points** on a 50/50 gamble. Win and you double your stake. Lose and it's gone. |
| **Soul Burst** | Absorbs **25% of all non-question damage** taken while in your inventory. Activate to unleash the stored damage on a foe and heal yourself for the same amount. |
| **Spiny Shell** | Hurl this devastating shell at whoever is in **1st place**. Deals damage equal to **20% of the leader's current points**. |
| **Thieving Rat** | The rat scurries into a foe's bag and returns with a mystery item. Activate to **steal a random card** from an opponent. |
| **Traveling Merchant** | A mysterious trader offers you four wares. Activate to view **4 random cards** and choose **1** to keep. |

Quests are special objectives given by the card **"Martin"**. Completing them grants massive rewards, often including permanent upgrades to your Actions.

| Quest Name | Objective | Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Seer Quest** | Use Mad Seer 5 times. | 125 Gold + **Mad Seer Upgrade** |
| **Jester's Quest** | Buy 3 cards from the Card Jester. | 200 Gold + **Card Jester Upgrade** |

## 7. Advanced Mechanics

### Spider Sense & Rerolls
*   **Spider Sense:** Gained by feeding the Spider. Increases the points you earn from correct answers (Multiplier).
*   **Rerolls:** Gained via Spider Sense levels. Allows you to "Reroll" your Black Market card choices if you don't like them.

### Puppet Lock
If a player uses a **Puppet Master** card on you, you are "Locked". On your next turn, you **must** choose a question from the category they selected. You cannot pick other categories until the lock is broken (by answering/passing the forced question).

### Freezing
*   **Frozen Tile:** Cannot be selected by anyone.
*   **Frozen Action:** Cannot be used by the target player.
*   Effect lasts until the start of the caster's next turn.

### Alliances
Created by the **Coalition** card. Allied players are visually linked.
*   You cannot target an ally with harmful cards (Loot Goblin, Spiny Shell, etc.).
*   Lasts for a set number of turns before expiring.

### Multipliers
Some tiles on the board may have hidden multipliers (x2, x3) that increase the point value of the question (and the penalty for a wrong answer!).

---

## 8. Winning the Game

The game ends when **all tiles** on the board have been cleared (answered or marked done).

*   **Winner:** The player with the highest positive score.
*   **Ties:** Shared victory (or sudden death if you decide to house-rule it!).
